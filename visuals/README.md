# Visuals

## Overview

This directory contains photographs, diagrams, flow visualizations, and video documentation of experimental setups and procedures.

---

## Content Categories

### Setup & Hardware
- Wind tunnel construction photos
- Instrumentation and sensor placement
- Model mounting and alignment
- Test section interior views

### Airfoil Models
- Close-up photography of test models
- Surface texture details
- Scale reference photos
- Comparison shots of different profiles

### Flow Visualization
- Smoke visualization tests
- Dye injection patterns
- Pressure distribution imagery
- Boundary layer separation visualization

### Flight Testing
- RC/drone platform photos
- In-flight camera footage
- Telemetry graph screenshots
- Test site documentation

### Graphs & Analysis
- Lift and drag curves
- Velocity profiles
- Efficiency comparisons
- Iteration progress comparisons

### Video Clips
- Wind tunnel operation (30–60 second clips)
- Flow visualization sequences
- Flight test footage
- Data collection processes

---

## Naming Convention

`[category]_[description]_[date].jpg`

Example: `wind_tunnel_front_view_2026-03-04.jpg`

---

## Quality Standards

- Professional composition and lighting
- Clear scale references where applicable
- High resolution (minimum 1280×720 for photos)
- Clear labels and annotations
- Proper file format (JPG for photos, PNG for diagrams)

---

## Why Visuals Matter

For admissions impact:
✅ Makes your work tangible and real  
✅ Breaks up text-heavy documentation  
✅ Shows professionalism and communication skills  
✅ Helps viewers understand complex concepts  

*A single compelling image can communicate more than pages of text.*
