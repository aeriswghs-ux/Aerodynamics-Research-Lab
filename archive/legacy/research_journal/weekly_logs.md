# Research Journal

## Weekly Logs & Progress Tracking

This journal documents the iterative research process — what was tried, what failed, what was learned, and how the work evolved.

---

## Purpose

- **Transparency:** Show real research thinking, not just polished results
- **Accountability:** Track time investment and effort
- **Learning:** Document insights and course corrections
- **Admissions Appeal:** Demonstrate genuine intellectual curiosity and problem-solving

---

## Weekly Entry Template

```
## Week of [Date]

### What I Worked On
- Specific tasks completed
- Time spent per activity

### What I Learned
- New insights about aerodynamics or experimental design
- Physics principles validated or challenged

### What Didn't Work (& Why)
- Failed approaches or experiments
- Root cause analysis
- Decision on whether to retry or pivot

### Next Week's Plan
- Refined hypothesis
- Modified approach
- New questions to investigate

### Questions That Came Up
- Open research questions
- Areas needing clarification
- Potential future investigations
```
