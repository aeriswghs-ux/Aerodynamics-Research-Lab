# Wind tunnel run log — blank template

[← Initial test record](README.md)

Copy this template for a future run. It is not a completed experiment. Replace each “Not recorded” only with an actual observation or measurement; use “Not applicable” with a reason when appropriate.

## Identification and question

- Run ID: Not recorded
- Test date, time, and timezone: Not recorded
- Operator: Not recorded
- Question/hypothesis: Not recorded
- Planned acceptance criteria and rationale: Not recorded
- Changed variable, fixed conditions, and repeat number: Not recorded

## Configuration

- Labeled setup photograph/diagram and revision: Not recorded
- Tunnel dimensions and airflow direction: Not recorded
- Fan model, setting, guard, and mounting: Not recorded
- Model identity, dimensions, orientation, and support: Not recorded
- Sensor identity, range, units, location, and sample interval: Not recorded
- Calibration/reference method, date, and uncertainty: Not recorded
- Ambient conditions: Not recorded
- Visualization material and introduction method, if used: Not recorded
- Camera position, lighting, and recording settings: Not recorded

## Procedure and evidence

- Fan-off baseline and tare procedure: Not recorded
- Stabilization criterion and actual recording interval: Not recorded
- Procedure performed, including deviations: Not recorded
- Raw data file(s): Not recorded
- Original video file and checksum: Not recorded
- Video timestamps corresponding to readings/observations: Not recorded
- Qualitative observations, with timestamps: Not recorded
- Corrections applied, rationale, and separate processed file(s): Not recorded

## Interpretation

- Measured results with units and uncertainty: Not recorded
- Comparison against predeclared criteria: Not recorded
- Repeatability across separately identified runs: Not recorded
- Limitations, possible confounders, and missing evidence: Not recorded
- Conclusion supported by the evidence: Not recorded
- Next action: Not recorded

Keep descriptive video observations separate from instrument measurements and hypotheses. Do not use example or simulated data as experimental results.
